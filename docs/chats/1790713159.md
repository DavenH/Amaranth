# Preset-specific surface styles

Date: 2026-09-29

## Context and decisions

The CPU lab recipes now provide the desired aesthetic. Preserve them exactly
when integrating; do not resume speculative shader tuning.

The canonical time-surface choices are Greyscale (straight, undistorted mapping),
Icy Hot (recipe 13), Blues (the original Cycle 1 gradient), and Bullion (the
recipe 14 direction, currently revised by recipe 20). Recipes 15–19 are preview
variants, not canonical. Keep experiments clearly separate in the menu.

Save the selected style in each preset: different sounds benefit from different
tradeoffs between colourful detail and low-frequency definition. Icy Hot and
Bullion may receive further user-authored low-frequency improvements; do not
invent those changes while porting the current saved recipes.
