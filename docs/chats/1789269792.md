# Live Preview Acceptance

Date: 2026-09-13

## Context

A Reverb preview fix was accepted after proving eventual content change in
`Live` probe mode, but the reported default-mode slider interaction remained
unresponsive.

## Decisions

- Keep the kernel-derived Reverb spectrogram as the only Reverb preview.
- Under `On Release`, update the edited node's local preview during the gesture
  while deferring only downstream traversal and Spy previews.
- Prove successive content changes while a real native drag remains held; a
  later update or mouse-up is not evidence for per-movement responsiveness.

## Generalized Rules

- Do not close a live-interaction regression with eventual-state evidence.
  Verify the first meaningful movement and at least one subsequent movement
  before release under the user's default settings.
- Treat refresh preferences according to their named scope. A downstream-probe
  policy must not silently suppress local editor feedback.
