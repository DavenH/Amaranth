# Cycle V2 Visual Convergence

Date: 2026-09-20

## Context

Cycle V2 mockups explored how the graph workspace could adopt the preset
browser's refined deep-navy, cyan-focus, and spectral-preview design language.

## Decisions

- Prefer a unified top bar that combines document status, transport, keyboard,
  zoom, search, and related global actions.
- Retain subtle bright boundary highlights on piano keys, graph nodes, and
  envelope edit regions; use them as restrained edge definition rather than a
  broad glow effect.
- Keep the richer preset-browser panel treatment and spectral thumbnail style.
- Preset search should narrow results through coalesced asynchronous filtering;
  Return loads the highlighted preset.
- Give every workspace an implicit default output spy. A single click toggles
  its Time/Spectrum view without adding a serialized graph probe.
- Define that default observation point after semantically visual global effects
  such as Waveshaper and IR Modeller, but before trailing Reverb, Delay, and EQ.
- Store normalized spectral preset previews as compact JPEG presentation
  metadata in the preset JSON so browsing never needs to render audio eagerly.
- Treat the bottom spy cards as signal observations at graph processing stages,
  not as preset cards. Do not invent preset names for them.
- Keep spy cards numbered without persistent names. Reveal graph source or
  processing-stage identity contextually when orientation requires it.
- Render bipolar Trimesh information with a warm-positive, dark-neutral,
  cool-negative gradient. The neutral midpoint should read as a stable identity
  boundary, not as another saturated colour.
- Explore a perspectival Trimesh surface editor that supports orbiting or flying
  around editable waveform geometry while retaining an exact linked 2D slice.
- Improve the orthographic top-down time-surface heatmap so subtle IR-modelled
  ripples, ridges, and valleys remain visible in compact previews and the
  expanded 2D panel. This relief treatment is separate from the perspectival
  3D editor concept.

## Generalized Rules

- Let spectral imagery carry information and character while surrounding chrome
  stays quiet and precisely aligned.
- Use luminous edges sparingly to clarify boundaries, focus, and editable
  regions.
- Never apply library or preset semantics to signal-monitoring surfaces merely
  because they share a thumbnail-card visual treatment.
- Make bipolarity geometric as well as chromatic in 3D views: positive and
  negative geometry should cross a visible zero plane.
- Keep navigable 3D editing grounded with reset/focus actions, an orientation
  reference, and a linked slice so camera freedom does not obscure exact values.
- Preserve value colour as the semantic truth, then reveal geometry with
  normals, soft directional light, restrained specularity, and cavity shadow.
  Lighting must not move the bipolar neutral boundary or imply different data.
- Keep heatmap relief strictly top-down and rectangular: derive pseudo-normals,
  slope, and curvature from neighbouring scalar samples without displacing the
  surface or introducing perspective.
- Do not add roughness, noise, micro-ripples, or terrain-like texture to authored
  Trimesh heatmaps. Curve-editor surfaces should retain their few smooth folds;
  Mathematica-like shading may reveal those derivatives but must not invent
  complexity more appropriate to a downstream Spy after FX processing.
- Prefer smooth high-resolution Mathematica-like shading over visible low-poly
  patches. Map signed amplitude monotonically from deep blue troughs through a
  low-chroma charcoal zero region to orange peaks; use absolute magnitude to
  increase saturation and value away from zero.
- Keep derivative detail subordinate to amplitude: gate edge accents to genuine
  high-slope or high-curvature regions, keep them narrow and low-strength, and
  never let them tint broad flat regions or compete with the bipolar reading.
  Either a restrained hue-diverging seam or a same-palette saturation/value
  lift can expose local IR detail. Specularity should remain smooth and
  continuous.

## Open Questions
