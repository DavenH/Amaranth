# Playback cursors and release previews

Date: 2026-10-24

## Context

The user requested Cycle 1 cursor behavior in Cycle 2 mesh and Envelope panels,
including morph activity, scratch traversal, and voice ADSR loop/release state.

## Decisions

- Envelope playback time is Phase, independent of its Yellow morph coordinate.
- The initial 80/20 proposal was superseded on 2026-10-25: fit voice duration
  plus the longest envelope release, with a variable split. Hold the unscratched
  waveshape at x=1 during release.
- Continue sampling Guide noise during that hold; do not freeze noise with the
  morph coordinate.

## Generalized Rules

Read cursor positions from actual audio playback and resolved scratch/morph
owners. Keep visual cursor animation ephemeral and free of document publication
or surface rebuilding. Do not infer playback state from wall-clock time in UI.
