# Explicit Trimesh Types And Parked Multi-Oscillator Semantics

Date: 2026-09-13

## Context

Removing routine Voice Context cables exposed undefined semantics for combining
cycle-based oscillator regions at different pitches, especially in spectral
space.

## Decisions

- Treat multiple Voice Contexts as independent oscillators, but table their
  cross-context spectral and semiglobal modulation semantics until the phase,
  pitch, materialization, and polyphony boundaries are designed.
- Make Trimesh signal type explicit as Time, Magnitude, or Phase.
- Make magnitude polarity explicit as Unipolar or Bipolar and independent of
  Add/Multiply graph operations. Do not retain an Auto mode.
- Preserve Cycle 1 migration as additive/unipolar and multiply/bipolar while
  allowing every polarity/arithmetic combination in new Cycle V2 graphs.
- With one Voice Context, infer oscillator properties without visible or
  serialized source-context cables. With several contexts, require explicit
  assignment and temporarily allow only one context to participate.
- Keep the stored spectral-magnitude grid in its native range. Have the graph
  compiler derive an operation-specific transfer for each consumer edge from
  the source polarity and receiving Add/Multiply operation; do not constrain a
  source to one downstream consumer merely to make its scaling unambiguous.

## Generalized Rules

- Persist user-authored signal meaning on the source node instead of inferring
  it from graph position or a configuration node.
- Keep value range/polarity independent from the arithmetic operation consuming
  the values.
- Do not apply consumer-dependent scaling at a shared source. Preserve source
  data and lower the required transfer independently at each edge.
- Do not approximate undefined multi-oscillator DSP. Enforce a narrow temporary
  boundary and preserve the proposal for later design.

## Open Questions

- Where independent oscillator outputs become a per-synth-voice semiglobal
  signal before polyphonic voice summation.
- Which cross-context operations are meaningful, especially spectral arithmetic
  between contexts at different pitches.
