# Follow visual references without restyling them

Date: 2026-11-14

## Context

The user asked for awkward prompts to look like the existing movie exporter or
preset-save dialog. The initial implementation incorrectly restyled the exporter
and introduced rounded fields/buttons.

## Decisions

The named dialogs are targets to preserve and reuse. The user prefers their
existing square-edged Amaranth controls over the proposed rounded treatment.
For the prompts, retain the app’s dark teal background and blue fill on the
currently focused button; the movie exporter remains unchanged.

## Generalized Rules

When asked to make A look like B, preserve B and reuse its actual presentation
owner. Do not redesign the reference or substitute a new aesthetic. Treat an
explicit reference as a constraint, not general inspiration.
